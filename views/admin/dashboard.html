Stock

Upload your price list (CSV)
Columns: brand, model, price, category, condition, storage, color, stock, imageUrl (optional). Category can be Phones, Accessories, Tablets, Audio, or anything else you want — leave blank and it defaults to Phones. Re-uploading updates matching rows instead of duplicating them.
Bulk photo upload
Select as many photos as you like at once. Name each file exactly like the device: brand, model, storage and colour (if it has one), e.g. apple-iphone-13-128gb.jpg or samsung-galaxy-s21-plus-128gb.jpg (a + in a name is written plus). Add -2, -3 up to -5 for extra photos of the same device (apple-iphone-13-128gb-2.jpg). The first photo is the main one. Photos are resized for you. Use JPG or PNG (iPhone users: set Camera to Most Compatible, or share as JPG).

Referrals

Rule: a referral is confirmed only after the order is paid and the payment has cleared. The referrer is paid 48 hours after you confirm. When you confirm, enter the gross profit left on the sale after any discount: R1000+ pays R300, R700 to R999 pays R200, R500 to R699 pays R150, under R500 pays R0. Pay by hand (EFT or cash), then press Mark paid. Confirm and pay from the Orders tab.

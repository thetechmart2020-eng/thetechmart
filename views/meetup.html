Book a meetup

Pick the device, a date and a time slot, and tell us where you would like to meet. We confirm on WhatsApp.

Meetups are on Saturdays and Sundays only and need 2 days' notice so we can get the device to our sales agent.

How meetups work

  1. Send your request. It opens WhatsApp (or email) with the details filled in. Press send.
  2. We release the device from the warehouse and send it to our sales agent. This is why we need 2 days' notice.
  3. We confirm the time and place with you on WhatsApp.
  4. A meetup costs R200 on top of the device price, because it is a special request outside our normal sales process. Collection from North Riding / Kya Sands is R0.
  5. Pay by Yoco or EFT before the meetup, or pay cash at the meetup.
  6. The R200 meetup fee cannot be cancelled or refunded once the device is with our sales agent.

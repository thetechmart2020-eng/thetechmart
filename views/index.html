FAQ

Questions, answered

What warranty do I get?

Standard-price devices: 3-month repair or replacement warranty. Negotiated devices (special-price devices): 21-day replacement warranty.

What do Grade A-B mean?

Our devices are Grade A-B pre-owned and quality checked. The condition of each device is shown on its card and page.

How long does delivery take?

The Courier Guy delivery: flat R100, about 2 days. Delivery R100 flat.

Can I make an offer on the price?

Yes. Tap Make an offer on any device and we reply on WhatsApp.

Can I sell or trade in my device?

Yes. Use the Sell / Trade-in form with your device details and photos, and we reply on WhatsApp.

What if there is a problem with my device?

WhatsApp us on 071 662 3565 with a photo or video showing the problem. We'll send a reference number and an estimated resolution. Most issues are resolved within about 7 days. This does not limit your statutory consumer rights. Returns, warranty, delivery and payment

How can I pay?

Pay by Yoco (card) or EFT. Cash on collection (meetup or pickup only), never for courier delivery.

Can I meet up to collect my device?

Yes. Book a meetup. Meetups are on Saturdays and Sundays only, cost R200 on top of the device price (a special request outside our normal sales process) and need 2 days' notice so we can get the device to our sales agent. The meetup fee cannot be refunded once the device is with our sales agent. Collection from North Riding / Kya Sands is R0. You can pay by Yoco or EFT before the meetup, or pay cash at the meetup.

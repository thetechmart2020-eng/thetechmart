To log an issue, WhatsApp us on 071 662 3565 with a photo or video showing the problem. We send a reference number and an estimated resolution, and most issues are resolved within about 7 days.

Selling or trading in a device

Sending us photos and details is a request, not a sale. Any price we reply with depends on the device matching the description and photos you sent.

Referrals

You earn R300 for each referred purchase, once the buyer's payment has cleared and we have confirmed the sale. We pay about 48 hours after we confirm. You cannot refer yourself, and we may decline a referral that does not look genuine.
